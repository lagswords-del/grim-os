TROUBLESHOOTING

Black screen after login
  Press Ctrl+Alt+F2, log in, and run  journalctl -b -p err
  NVIDIA cards: try installing nvidia-open (RTX/GTX 16 and newer).

Wrong password at the login screen
  The text login remembers the last username, so check the
  Username line before typing your password. Grim OS does not lock
  your account after wrong tries.

No sound
  systemctl --user restart pipewire wireplumber

Update failed with "signature" or "keyring" errors
  sudo pacman -Sy archlinux-keyring && update

Out of disk space
  sudo pacman -Sc      clears old downloaded packages

Install log
  The installer's log is saved at /var/log/griminstall.log
