DESKTOPS AND SHORTCUTS

Pick your desktop on the login screen (F3 on the text login screen).

The same keys work on every desktop (Super is the Windows key):
  Super+T            toggle floating
                     (IceWM, Openbox, XFCE, GNOME and KDE always float,
                      so there Super+T maximizes / restores instead)
  Super+Q            close the window
  Super+Space        find an app
  Super+Return       open a terminal
  Super+F1           Grim Settings (rices, bar, dock)
  Super+Shift+E      power menu
  Print              screenshot: drag over an area
  Super+left drag    move a window
  Super+right drag   resize a window

Tiling window managers (i3, Sway, Hyprland, Niri) also have
  Super+1..9 workspaces, Super+arrows to move focus,
  Super+Shift+arrows to move windows, Super+F fullscreen.

Bars: Polybar on i3, Openbox and IceWM, Waybar on Sway, Hyprland and Niri,
the built-in panel on XFCE, GNOME, KDE and COSMIC. Bar styles (Grim Settings > Bars):
Classic, Reaper Pills, Red Island, Nord Pills, Frost Glass, Graphite Icons, CachyOS Minimal.
Dock: Plank (X11), nwg-dock (Wayland), Dash to Panel (GNOME).
Turn the dock on or off in Grim Settings.

Rices: Grim (black and red), Nord Snow (cold blue) and Bone (light).
Apply one in Grim Settings, or in a terminal:  grim-rice apply nord-snow
Your own config files are kept next to the new ones as *.pre-grim.

GRIM DE (the Grim desktop)
  Pick "Grim" on the login screen. It's niri (scrolling tiling, blur)
  with Grim Shell (bar and dock). Settings: Super+F1 or Grim DE Settings.
  Super+G turns game mode on and off: no blur, no transparency, no
  animations, for the best frame rates. Games always run solid anyway.
  Your own niri additions go in ~/.config/grim-de/niri/custom.kdl.
  Bar: click any icon for a small menu. The sliders icon is the control
  center (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Do Not Disturb, game mode, volume, brightness,
  what's playing). The bell is the notification centre. An arrow icon
  shows up when updates are ready.
  Bar layouts (Grim DE Settings > Bar > Layout): full strip, floating glass,
  three pills, island, minimal, Windows 7 taskbar. Time and date are
  separate: show either or both. Workspaces as dots or numbers.
  Dock (Grim DE Settings > Dock): pick the style, the screen edge, and
  build it from items (apps, app finder, workspaces, window title,
  clock, date, separators, gaps, your own buttons), each in its own pill
  if you like.
  Hide a window into the dock: click its app in the dock while it's
  focused, or press Super+Shift+M. Click the app again to bring it back.
  Windows 7 look: Grim DE Settings > Windows > Windows 7 style.
  grimfetch colours: Grim DE Settings > Terminal (or Grim Settings).

UPDATES AND DOWNLOADS
  update             update everything
  update --mirrors   pick the fastest download mirrors again
                     (also done every week, and by update when the list is old)
  pacman downloads 10 packages at once, shows colour and a Pac-Man bar,
  keeps the last 2 versions of each package, and tells you when a reboot
  is needed or when packages aren't needed any more.
