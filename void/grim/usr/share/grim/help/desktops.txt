Desktops
========
Grim Void has no login screen: log in on tty1 and your desktop starts
("desktop =" in /etc/grim/system.conf, or grimhelp > Desktop at login).

  IceWM     light, with a taskbar and start menu (X11)
  Niri      scrolling tiling, Waybar on top (Wayland)
  Openbox   floating, right-click the desktop for a menu (X11)
  i3        tiling with a bar (X11)
  dwm       tiny tiling, status on the right (X11)
  river     tiling, Waybar on top (Wayland)

  grim-session            pick one now
  grim-session i3         start that one
  grimctl wallpaper set ~/Pictures/wall.png     the wallpaper, on any of them
Keys: grimhelp keys
