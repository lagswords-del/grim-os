grim-dock (the IceWM taskbar)
=============================
Install it from grimhelp > Install apps > Grim apps (or Quick settings > Taskbar look).

  grim-dock              the menu: modes, build your own, back to Grim's bar or IceWM's own
  grim-dock dock         switch straight to a mode
  grim-dock show         the current settings

Modes:
  grim       Grim's full-width bar (the default)
  dock       a centred bar with big icons, no titles
  floating   a centred dock that hides until the mouse touches the bottom
  minimal    workspaces, windows and the clock
  top        the Grim bar at the top
  win7       big grouped icons and a show-desktop strip
  stats      CPU, RAM, network and battery graphs
  icewm      IceWM's own taskbar and stock theme

Build your own: position, width, alignment, size, auto-hide, titles, icons, grouping, start
button, window list, show-desktop button, workspaces, clock, tray, graphs, battery, colour.
It writes ~/.icewm/prefoverride (lines you add above grim-dock's are kept) and restarts IceWM
in place, so your windows stay open.
