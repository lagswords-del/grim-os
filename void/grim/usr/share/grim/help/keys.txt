Keys (the same on every desktop)
================================
  Super + Enter        terminal
  Super + Q            close the window
  Super + F            fullscreen on/off
  Super + T            floating on/off   (IceWM and Openbox float anyway: maximise on/off)
  Super + D            app launcher
  Super + W            Wi-Fi
  Super + 1..9         workspace / tag
  Super + Shift + 1..9 move the window there
  Super + Shift + E    log out
  Print                quick screenshot to ~/Pictures
  Super + Shift + S    Grim Shot: area, window, full, record, GIF (install it in grimhelp)
  Super + left drag    move a window      (IceWM)
  Super + right drag   resize a window    (IceWM)

dwm also:  Super+J/K focus, Super+H/L resize, Super+Shift+Enter swap with main,
           Super+Shift+T tile, Super+Shift+F floating layout, Super+M monocle, Super+B bar
niri also: Super+arrows focus, Super+R column width, Super+O overview
