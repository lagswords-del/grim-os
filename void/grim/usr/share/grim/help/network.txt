Network and Wi-Fi
=================
  grim-wifi              finds your Wi-Fi card, says if a driver or firmware is
                         missing, lists networks by number and connects
  grim-wifi --detect     only the hardware report
  nmtui                  NetworkManager's own menus (VPNs, static IPs...)
A cable connects by itself.
Missing firmware? sudo grimctl install linux-firmware-network  (cable first)
