grim-spotify
============
Spotify in the terminal, in Grim's colours, with cover art and lyrics.
Install it from grimhelp (Music and video) or: sudo grimctl install spotify-player-grim
Needs Spotify Premium (Spotify's rule for every player that isn't theirs).

  grim-spotify            open it (the first time it signs you in)
  grim-spotify keys       the key list
  grim-spotify next / prev / play     control it from another terminal or a keybind

  space play/pause   n/p next/previous   >/< fast forward/back   +/- volume
  l lyrics   u p playlists   g y liked songs   / search   z queue   ? every key
